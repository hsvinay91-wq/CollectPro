How it works

Add each customer with loan amount, weekly amount and total payable weeks. Total Payable = Per Week × Total Weeks. Paid Amount = Sum of actual payments, and Remaining = Total Payable − Paid Amount. After every payment, the next week is shown automatically. When all payable weeks are paid, the account becomes Completed and no extra week is added until you use Add Week. Data is saved locally in this browser.
