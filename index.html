How it works

Add each customer with weekly amount and total payable weeks. Total Payable = Weekly Amount × Total Weeks. Paid Amount = Weekly Amount × Paid Weeks, and Remaining = Total Payable − Paid Amount. Use Pay to record the next week or Miss when a week is unpaid. Everything is saved locally in this browser.
