Collection rules

Total Payable = Per Week × Payable Weeks.
Paid Amount = sum of actual payment entries.
Paid Weeks = payment entries marked as paid + any historical paid weeks entered during setup.
The loan date never makes an account Completed. Completion happens only when paid weeks reach payable weeks.

Add Customer / Existing Customer

Why these historical fields?
For an old customer, enter the weeks already paid before using CollectPro. These old weeks count toward completion, but the system will NOT invent fake dates or fake payment rows. You can then add actual old payment rows from the customer page whenever you have the dates/details.

Add Payment

For old data, choose the old payment date and week number. This payment will be added to the existing history and counted only once as a paid week.
